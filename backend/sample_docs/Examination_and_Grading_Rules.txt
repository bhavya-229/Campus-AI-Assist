================================================================================
EXAMINATION RULES, RE-EVALUATION & GRADING GUIDELINES
================================================================================
Document Code: EXAM-GUIDE-2026

1. EXAMINATION PROTOCOL & HALL TICKETS
- Hall tickets (Admit Cards) are issued 7 days prior to examination commencement through the student portal.
- Students must bring their official College ID Card and printed Hall Ticket to all examination sessions.
- Electronic gadgets, programmable calculators, and smartwatches are strictly prohibited in the exam hall.

2. RE-EVALUATION & ANSWER SCRIPT VIEWING
2.1 Procedure:
Students dissatisfied with their Semester End Examination results may apply for photocopy viewing and re-evaluation within 10 days of result publication.
2.2 Fee Structure:
- Answer Script Photocopy: Rs. 300 per subject.
- Re-evaluation / Re-totalling: Rs. 500 per subject.
- If re-evaluation results in an increase of >=15% marks, 50% of the re-evaluation fee will be refunded to the student's registered bank account.

3. SUPPLEMENTARY / BACKLOG EXAMINATIONS
- Supplementary examinations are conducted once every academic year during the summer break (July - August).
- Maximum of 4 backlog subjects can be attempted in a single supplementary exam window.

4. 10-POINT GRADING SCALE
- Grade 'O' (Outstanding): 90 - 100 Marks (Grade Points: 10)
- Grade 'A+' (Excellent): 80 - 89 Marks (Grade Points: 9)
- Grade 'A' (Very Good): 70 - 79 Marks (Grade Points: 8)
- Grade 'B+' (Good): 60 - 69 Marks (Grade Points: 7)
- Grade 'B' (Above Average): 55 - 59 Marks (Grade Points: 6)
- Grade 'C' (Pass): 45 - 54 Marks (Grade Points: 5)
- Grade 'F' (Fail): Below 45 Marks (Grade Points: 0)
